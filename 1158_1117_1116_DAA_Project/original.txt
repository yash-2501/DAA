Algorithms are step by step procedures for solving problems. The design and analysis of algorithms studies how to create efficient procedures and how to measure their running time and memory usage. Divide and conquer breaks a problem into smaller subproblems, solves them independently and combines the results. Greedy algorithms make the locally best choice at each step hoping to reach a global optimum. Dynamic programming stores the answers of overlapping subproblems so that they are never computed twice. String matching algorithms search for a pattern inside a larger text and are used in editors, search engines and plagiarism checkers.
